05 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions
to ask first.

Each workshop listing will answer the practical details before you decide.

Ask about a session →
Is a workshop suitable for beginners?

Check the experience level on the individual workshop page. It should clearly explain whether beginners are welcome and what, if anything, you should know beforehand.

What materials will I need?

A confirmed workshop listing will include a supply list and explain whether you need to bring materials yourself.

Will I receive recordings or feedback?

Replay access and feedback vary by programme. These will be stated explicitly on each listing rather than assumed to be included.

How do I know a booking is confirmed?

Registration will open only when the schedule, fee, booking process and terms are confirmed. A booking should be considered confirmed only after the owner or booking service confirms it.

Can I ask about a private group or collaboration?

Yes. Send a short enquiry describing your idea, group size if relevant and preferred timing. Availability will be confirmed directly.
