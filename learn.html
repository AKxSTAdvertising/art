01 / THE EXPERIENCE

A little more colour
in your everyday.

Watercolour journaling brings observation, sketching and paint together on the page. The aim isn't to make every page perfect. It's to learn the process, notice more of the world around you and grow comfortable through practice.

This page is a preview of the workshop experience. Confirmed session dates, teaching details and enrolment terms will be added before bookings open.

02 / YOUR LEARNING JOURNEY

From first washes to
pages that tell a story.

A sample learning path showing how a watercolour journaling programme can build gradually. The final syllabus will be confirmed with the workshop details.

PART 01 · FOUNDATIONS

BEGIN WITH THE BASICS

Sketching & watercolour foundations

Get familiar with your tools, observe simple shapes, explore line and try the first watercolour techniques.

  • Materials and page setup
  • Shapes, proportion and observation
  • Water, pigment, washes and texture
PART 02 · EXPLORE

NOTICE THE EVERYDAY

Colour, composition & journaling

Try colour combinations, arrange a page and turn familiar objects or small moments into creative prompts.

  • Colour mixing and simple palettes
  • Page composition and lettering
  • Everyday objects and loose studies
PART 03 · MAKE IT YOURS

BRING IT TOGETHER

Scenes, atmosphere & personal pages

Bring observation, line and colour together, practise depth and begin developing a page-making rhythm of your own.

  • Foreground, background and depth
  • Simple scenes and visual storytelling
  • Ideas for continued independent practice

Illustrative curriculum only. Exact lesson topics, session count and teaching schedule are not confirmed yet.
