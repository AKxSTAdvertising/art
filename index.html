GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be wondering.

Ask a question →
Do I need any art experience to join a workshop?

Workshop requirements will vary. Each workshop page will explain the intended experience level, materials and what to expect before you book.

How do I find out about upcoming workshops?

Visit the Workshops page for current details. Dates and booking information will be published once they are confirmed.

Can I ask about a painting before buying?

Absolutely. Use the contact page to ask about a piece, its dimensions, availability, delivery and care. Availability and prices will be confirmed directly.

Can we talk about a custom project or collaboration?

Yes—share a little about your idea through the contact page and request a conversation. We’ll confirm whether the project is a fit.
