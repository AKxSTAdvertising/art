BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Questions, answered.

A few helpful starting points. Specific workshop and artwork details will appear on their own pages.

Ask us something →
Do I need experience to join an art workshop?

It depends on the workshop. Each listing will explain the intended experience level, format and materials so you can decide whether it fits you.

Where can I see upcoming workshops?

Visit the Workshops page for confirmed formats and booking information. Dates and availability will be published once finalised.

Can I ask questions about a painting before buying?

Yes. Use the contact page to ask about the specific piece, dimensions, medium, price, availability and delivery. Details will be confirmed before a sale.

Can I book a call about a collaboration or custom idea?

Yes. Send a short description of your idea through the contact page. A call is not confirmed until a time has been agreed directly.
